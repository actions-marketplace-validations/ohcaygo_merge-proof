5. **P2 — Ledger labels delivery-time observations as merge-decision facts.** `github/service.js:539-568` and `github/ledger.js:53-56`. recordMerge selects the newest matching-head receipt without constraining issuedAt to merged_at, and computes its gate with today's stored policy/currentness. Do not silently promote local delivery-time knowledge to a decision-point fact.
